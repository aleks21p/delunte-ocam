The five CSV files are unchanged extracts from data/suite in that archive.
Haffop displays a subset of columns. No endorsement by the creators is implied.
The source aggregates public OpenBenchmarking.org results; these are historical
community measurements, not manufacturer specifications or health guarantees.
The CSVs omit exact workload versions, options, units and system configurations.
Consequently Haffop does not compare these numbers to its own microbenchmarks,
infer missing units, or award "meets factory standard" ratings from them.
Coverage is limited to hardware present in the 2021 snapshot. Wi-Fi is not covered.
All browsing works offline. Haffop does not upload hardware data or scores.
